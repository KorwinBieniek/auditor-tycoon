# Sounds and music (all CC0, public domain)

- Interface sounds (click, sms ping, bell, pop): "Interface Sounds" by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0, via github.com/Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds
- Paper, coins, door: "50 RPG sound effects" by Kenney, CC0, opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects
- Coin, bubble: "RPG Sound Pack" by artisticdude, CC0, opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack
- Music: "Chill (Loopable)" by Pro Sensory, CC0, opengameart.org/node/145633
- Music: "Lofi Hip Hop Loop" by omfgdude, CC0, opengameart.org/content/lofi-hip-hop-loop

Files were converted to mono or stereo MP3 and loudness-normalised with ffmpeg. Credit is not required by CC0; it is given here anyway.
- Typing bursts: built from "UI Audio" switch clicks by Kenney (kenney.nl), CC0, via github.com/Calinou/kenney-ui-audio, mixed and softened with ffmpeg.
- Missed fraud tone: two soft notes generated with ffmpeg for this game, public domain.
